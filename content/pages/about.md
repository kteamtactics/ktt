## Who is behind KTT?

My name is Ugo. I'm a French journalist with several years of experience in sports journalism.

KTT is an independent project dedicated to understanding and improving at Kill Team. Through tactical breakdowns, battle reports, team analysis and practical tools, the goal is simple: look beyond the dice, understand the decisions that shape a game, and help players make better ones.

It's also where I bring together two things I enjoy: journalism and Kill Team. A place to investigate, experiment, share insights and learn along the way.

Built by a player, for players. Always evolving, always learning.

## What you will find here

- **Tactics**: one concept per article, with a board and a puzzle to train your eye.
- **Battle analysis**: real tournament games, explained in a few key moments.
- **Teams**: every kill team, its strengths, its weaknesses and its rules in plain words.
- **News**: what is coming next for Kill Team.
