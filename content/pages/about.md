## Who is behind KTT?

My name is Ugo. I spent eight years as a journalist (So Foot, Konbini, Chilowé, L'Éclaireur Fnac, La Dépêche du Midi), then five years as a primary school teacher. Today I spend a good part of my spare time on a 30 by 22-inch board, pushing small operatives around.

KTT is where I put what I learn along the way.

## What you will find here

- **Tactics**: one concept per article, with a board and a puzzle to train your eye.
- **Battle analysis**: real tournament games, explained in a few key moments.
- **Teams**: every kill team, its strengths, its weaknesses and its rules in plain words.
- **News**: what is coming next for Kill Team.
